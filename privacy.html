Motio privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Motio is a personal desktop app used only by its owner. It has no other users and no server of its own that collects data.

Google user data

  • Motio asks for one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, Motio can only see and change the files it created itself in the signed-in Google Drive. It cannot read any other file in that Drive.
  • Motio uses it to upload the videos, voice tracks and pictures it made, to check that each upload is complete, to play them back, to download them again when a video is redone, and to move them to the Drive trash when a project is deleted.
  • The sign-in tokens are kept only in a settings file on the owner's own computer. They are not sent anywhere except to Google.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files Motio made stay in the Drive until they are deleted there.
